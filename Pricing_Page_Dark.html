❓ Frequently Asked Questions ▼

What files do you convert?

We convert Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files to editable Word documents, PDFs, Canva, Affinity, and other formats. We preserve all formatting, images, and layouts.

How long does conversion take?

Standard timeline is 5-7 business days after we receive your files. Rush options available for urgent projects.

Should I schedule a consultation?

Yes! Consultations are free and help us understand your unique needs. Highly recommended for complex projects or if you're unsure which package fits.

What's the difference between packages?

Basic: Just the conversion. Standard: Includes data entry and automation. Premium: Full service customized software connection, training, and support.

How do I pay?

25% deposit to start after consult and plan, another 50% upon delivery, and balance upon satisfaction. We accept PayPal, bank transfers (Zelle, Venmo, etc.), credit cards, and checks.

What if I need revisions?

Unlimited revisions are included! If you want changes, just let us know and we'll update your files at no cost.
